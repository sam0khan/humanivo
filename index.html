Why AI writing sounds like AI

Language models default to predictable rhythm: even sentence lengths, the same handful of transition words, and a tone that's polished but flat. That pattern is exactly what AI detectors and readers alike learn to spot. Humanize rewrites your text with varied sentence structure, natural phrasing, and the small imperfections real writing has — while keeping your original meaning intact.

It's built for students checking their own drafts, writers cleaning up AI-assisted first passes, and anyone who wants their writing to read like a person wrote it — because it did, with some help.

Common questions

Is this actually free?

Yes. No signup, no subscription, no word limits behind a paywall. Paste text, get results.

Does this bypass AI detectors?

It rewrites text to sound naturally human, which often reduces AI-detection scores as a side effect. It's not designed or guaranteed to defeat any specific detector, and we'd encourage using AI-assisted writing responsibly and disclosing it where required.

Will this change the meaning of my text?

No — it rewrites phrasing, sentence rhythm, and word choice while preserving your original meaning and key facts.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Text is processed to generate your result and is not saved or logged. See our privacy policy for details.

Is there a character limit?

Very long inputs may take longer to process, but there's no hard paywall limit.
