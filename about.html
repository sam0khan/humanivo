About Sher Gul

Who Is Sher Gul?

Founder / Creator of Humanivo

Sher Gul is an accomplished customer support, sales, and digital marketing professional based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. He is the founder and creator of Humanivo, a digital AI writing tool built to help writers, creators, students, and professionals make AI-generated text sound more natural, polished, and human-readable.

With experience across both B2B and B2C environments, Sher specializes in lead generation, appointment setting, telemarketing, client outreach, customer relations, and growth-focused communication. His goal with Humanivo is to make writing improvement simple, fast, and accessible for anyone who wants cleaner, smoother, and more professional content.

Professional Experience

Sher has worked in roles including Sales Executive, Customer Support Executive, and Customer Relations Specialist with organizations such as Tribe Consulting, Raast Solutions, and Touchstone Communications.

Core Skills

His core competencies include B2B and B2C sales, lead generation, appointment setting, telemarketing, customer support, client relationship management, and digital marketing.

Freelance & Consulting

Sher is active on freelance platforms such as Upwork, offering support in digital marketing, lead generation, client outreach, and project collaboration, including work connected with Medinest Billing & IT Consultancy.

Background & Languages

Sher completed his matriculation, 10th grade, from Motherly Academy School in 2014. He is multilingual, with native fluency in Urdu and Pashto, professional fluency in English, and spoken proficiency in Punjabi. This communication background helps him connect with clients, teams, and audiences across different markets and cultures.

Digital Presence & Creative Projects

Sher manages digital branding and content creation across major platforms including Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok under the handle @shergulofficial. He also creates storytelling and narrative video content, including the Facebook page Legends & Lessons, focused on historical, moral, and educational stories.

What Humanivo Stands For

Humanivo was created with one simple idea: AI can help people write faster, but the final words should still feel human. The platform focuses on clarity, readability, natural flow, and practical writing improvement so users can turn robotic drafts into polished content with confidence.
