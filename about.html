About Humanivo

Writing That Still Sounds Like You

Humanivo is an AI text humanizer built for one job: taking stiff, repetitive AI writing and turning it into something a person would actually write.

Why Humanivo Exists

Built by Sher Gul

AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude are great at getting words on the page fast, but the output often reads flat, repetitive, or a little too polished in a way that feels obviously written by a machine. Humanivo rewrites that text so the sentence rhythm, wording, and flow feel closer to how a person actually writes, without changing what you were trying to say.

It was built by Sher Gul, a sales and client communication professional who spends most days writing outreach messages, follow up messages, and client facing content by hand. That daily experience with what natural writing sounds like, versus what AI writing sounds like, shaped how Humanivo's three transformation modes work.

Real World Writing Background

Built on experience writing sales outreach, client emails, and follow up messages across B2B and B2C roles at organizations including Tribe Consulting, Raast Solutions, and Touchstone Communications. This is work where natural, human wording directly affects whether someone replies.

What Shapes the Tool

Lead generation, appointment setting, telemarketing, and client relationship work all depend on writing that doesn't sound scripted. That standard is what Humanivo's Standard, Advanced, and Aggressive modes are tuned against.

Ongoing Work

Sher continues freelance work in digital marketing, lead generation, and client outreach, including collaboration with Medinest Billing & IT Consultancy, and applies lessons from that work back into Humanivo.

Background & Languages

Sher is based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, and is multilingual, with native fluency in Urdu and Pashto, professional fluency in English, and spoken proficiency in Punjabi. Working across languages and audiences day to day is part of what shaped Humanivo's focus on writing that reads naturally rather than mechanically, no matter who it's written for.

Beyond Humanivo

Sher also creates content under the handle @shergulofficial across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok, including Legends & Lessons, a storytelling project covering historical and moral narratives. Writing for an audience across formats, including video scripts, captions, and long form posts, is another place where "does this sound natural" matters as much as it does in Humanivo itself.

What Humanivo Stands For

AI can help you write faster, but the final result should still sound like you, not like a template. Humanivo focuses on three things: preserving your original meaning, improving sentence flow and rhythm, and giving you a result you'd actually feel comfortable sending, publishing, or submitting under your own name.
