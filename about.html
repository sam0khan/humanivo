About Humanivo

Useful tools with a human touch.

Humanivo is an independent collection of practical AI, text, developer, image and productivity tools. The mission is simple: remove friction from everyday digital work.

AI First

Humanivo's flagship experience is the AI Text Humanizer, built to help refine AI-assisted drafts into more natural writing.

Practical

Useful utilities live together in one clean workspace so small tasks do not require a pile of different websites.

Independent

Humanivo is built and maintained independently, with new tools and improvements added over time.
