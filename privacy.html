Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Humanivo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, AI writing tools, contact forms, and related pages.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, message details, or other information submitted through contact forms. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and general usage data.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate Humanivo, respond to inquiries, improve our tools, maintain website security, understand usage trends, and improve the user experience.

4. Text Submitted to Tools

Text submitted to Humanivo tools is processed to generate output. We do not intentionally retain submitted text after your session, unless required for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Humanivo may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand traffic, and analyze website performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Humanivo may use third-party services, including analytics providers and advertising networks such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent abuse.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through Humanivo. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. You should avoid submitting passwords, financial details, private records, or sensitive personal information into any online writing tool.

8. Children's Privacy

Humanivo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you, such as contact form submissions. You may also have the right to opt out of certain advertising or analytics features.

10. External Links

Our platform may contain links to external creator profiles, social networks, partner resources, or third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of those external websites.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Humanivo after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via email at shergulofficial@gmail.com.
